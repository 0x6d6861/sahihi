- You own the documents you upload or draft, and what signers submit. You allow us to store,

- You're responsible for having the right to send each document and to contact each recipient.
- Owners and admins control who joins the workspace and what each member can do.

## Acceptable use

Don't use Sahihi to break the law, to deceive the people you send documents to, to send spam, or
to try to get around its limits or security. We may suspend a workspace that does.

## Signatures and evidence

Sahihi records who signed, when and how they were identified, and produces a Certificate of
Completion. Whether an electronic signature is right for a particular document depends on the law
that applies to it. Sahihi doesn't give legal advice; check with a lawyer when you're unsure.

## Plans and limits

Each plan has monthly limits on envelopes and assistant replies and a number of seats, shown on
the pricing page and in your workspace settings. Limits reset on the 1st of each month, Nairobi
time. Paid plans are agreed with us directly until online payment is available.

## The drafting assistant

The assistant suggests wording and fills in blanks. Its suggestions can be wrong. Review the
document before you send it: you're responsible for what you send.

You can delete your workspace or your account at any time. We may end or suspend access for a
serious breach of these terms, and will tell you why.

The service is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to what
