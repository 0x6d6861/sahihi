# Privacy.

Last updated 13 October 2026.

> **Draft, under legal review.** This policy describes how the product works today. It is not yet
> final and may change before launch.

## Who we are

Sahihi is an electronic signature service for businesses. For the documents and signer details a
workspace puts into Sahihi, the workspace is the data controller and we process the data on its
behalf. For the accounts of the people who use Sahihi, we are the controller. Questions go to
[privacy@sahihi.co.ke](mailto:privacy@sahihi.co.ke).

## What we collect

- **Members of a workspace:** name, email address, profile picture and saved signature if added,
  sign-in details (password hash, passkeys, two-factor settings) and the workspace they belong to.
- **Documents:** the files a workspace uploads or drafts, and the fields placed on them.
- **Signers:** the name, email address and, if SMS codes are used, the phone number the sender
  enters; the signature, initials and values the signer submits; and, as evidence, the IP address,
  browser details and times of viewing, verifying and signing.
- **Signers who create an account (optional):** the same account details as members (name, email
  address, password hash and saved signature if added). The account shows the envelopes sent to
  its confirmed email address; workspaces can't see whether a signer has one.
- **This website** sets no cookies. It remembers a light or dark theme choice in your browser only.

## Why we use it

- To provide the service: deliver signing links and codes, collect signatures and produce the
  signed PDF.
- To keep evidence that a signature happened: the audit trail and the Certificate of Completion.
- To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and meet our legal obligations.

We don't sell personal data.

## Who processes it for us

- Cloud hosting, database and file storage providers that run Sahihi.
- Storage a workspace connects itself (its own bucket, Google Drive or OneDrive). Copies there are
  kept and deleted under that workspace's control: Sahihi never deletes them.
- An email delivery provider for signing links, codes and notifications.
- An SMS provider, Africa's Talking, when a sender asks for SMS codes.
- A model provider, only when a workspace has turned the drafting assistant on. Document text and
  chat messages for that workspace are then sent to it.

## How long we keep it

Each workspace chooses how long completed envelopes are kept and can delete them sooner. Deleting
removes files and signers' personal data. The document hashes and the audit trail are kept, because
they are the evidence that a signature happened, and the audit trail can't be edited afterwards.
Deleting your account removes your personal details while the workspace keeps its documents.

## Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal
data, and object to or restrict how it's used. If your data came to us through a workspace, we
may pass your request to that workspace. You can also complain to the Office of the Data
Protection Commissioner.

## Changes

We'll post any change to this policy on this page and update the date at the top.
